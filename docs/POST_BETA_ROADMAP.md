# CreatorJobs Post-Beta Roadmap

Date: 2026-06-02

This roadmap is intentionally not implemented for beta. These items should wait until real beta usage confirms demand and workflow shape.

| Item | Priority | User value | Complexity | Risk | Dependencies | Why after beta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Profile search | High | Recruiters can discover professionals beyond active listings. | Medium | Search quality and privacy expectations. | Public profile indexing, ranking, filters. | Beta should first validate jobs/talent listing liquidity. |
| Notification preferences | High | Users can control email/in-app noise. | Medium | Missed important marketplace events if defaults are wrong. | Notification categories and email templates. | Needs real notification volume data. |
| Admin moderation overhaul | High | Faster report review, safer marketplace operations. | High | Overbuilding before moderation patterns are known. | Admin roles, audit logs, moderation actions. | Beta manual review will reveal the right tools. |
| Real payment provider | High | Monetization and paid listings. | High | Payment compliance, refunds, failed payments. | Pricing model, receipts, idempotency, legal terms. | Beta currently uses launch-free entitlement mode. |
| Checkout receipts and invoices | Medium | Trust and accounting clarity. | Medium | Incorrect billing records. | Payment provider and entitlement model. | Should align with real payment integration. |
| Review system | Medium | Adds trust after completed collaborations. | High | Fake reviews, disputes, moderation burden. | Completed-work records and moderation. | Needs enough completed marketplace transactions. |
| Verified work samples | Medium | Recruiters trust portfolio claims more. | High | Verification complexity and false confidence. | Platform APIs, manual review, evidence policy. | Beta can validate which work samples matter most. |
| Content creator/channel verification | Medium | Helps identify legitimate hiring-side accounts. | Medium | OAuth edge cases and agency representation. | Identity linking, verification policy. | Existing identity scaffolding should be proven first. |
| Recommendation algorithm | Medium | Improves matching quality at scale. | High | Poor ranking can hide good listings. | Search analytics, apply/contact outcomes. | Requires beta interaction data. |
| Advanced filters | Medium | Faster narrowing by niche, platform, tools, budget, work mode. | Medium | Filter clutter. | Stable taxonomy and enough inventory. | Beta will show which filters users actually need. |
| Email digests | Medium | Brings users back to relevant jobs/talent. | Medium | Spammy cadence if poorly tuned. | Notification preferences, ranking. | Needs real posting/contact cadence. |
| Analytics dashboard | Low | Owners understand listing performance. | Medium | Dashboard/admin feel and metric misuse. | Event tracking and privacy policy. | Beta should first prove core posting value. |
| Recruiter outreach improvements | Medium | Better invites, follow-ups, and saved talent workflows. | Medium | Spam risk. | Rate limits, templates, invite states. | Contact quality should be observed in beta. |
| Talent availability calendar | Low | Makes capacity clearer for recruiters. | High | Calendar maintenance burden. | Booking/availability model. | Current beta should avoid fake scarcity. |
| Saved notes | Low | Recruiters can shortlist with private context. | Medium | Privacy and sync complexity. | Saved item metadata. | Useful after saved workflows show repeat usage. |
| Applicant pipeline | Medium | Hiring teams can manage candidates by stage. | High | Admin-dashboard complexity. | Applications, statuses, notifications. | Beta can validate simple status controls first. |
| Stronger marketplace ranking | High | Better quality surfacing as inventory grows. | High | Ranking bias and gaming. | Outcome data, moderation signals. | Requires real usage data. |
| AI-assisted matching | Low | Can suggest relevant jobs/talent faster. | High | Trust, hallucination, fairness, data privacy. | Rich profiles, applications, ranking baseline. | Should wait until deterministic marketplace workflows are mature. |
